## Brand & Style

This design system embodies high-end architectural modernism combined with contemporary editorial sophistication. The visual philosophy blends expansive cinematic perspective, sculptural geometric silhouettes, and tactile floating micro-elements.

### Key Tenets & Emotional Response
- **Sculptural & Spatial:** Evoking high-concept modernist villas, brutalist pavilions, and precision structural detailing. Interfaces feel like architectural blueprints brought to life with pristine lighting.
- **Editorial Fluidity:** Dynamic typographic pairing of an expressive, italicized serif with a hyper-disciplined geometric sans creates immediate luxury editorial authority.
- **Floating Tactility:** Semi-translucent glass pills, organic notched cutouts, and crisp stat badges float over atmospheric gradient sweeps and crisp, sunlit architectural volumes.
- **Warm Precision:** Grounded by deep nocturnal slates and crisp gallery whites, enriched with a refined terracotta-orange accent that injects warmth and decisive focus.

## Colors

The color system reconciles high-contrast architectural monumentality with editorial warmth:

- **Primary Accent (`#EE4D2D`):** A refined, vibrant terracotta-orange adapted for focal conversion points, callouts, active indicators, and brand anchoring.
- **Secondary Nocturne (`#0D1117`):** An abyssal slate-black used for architectural hero cards, high-contrast dark section surfaces, and dark-themed media frames.
- **Tertiary Horizon (`#2563EB`):** A vibrant cobalt blue representing the atmospheric sky and gradient wave backdrops shown in outdoor villa environments.
- **Neutral Core (`#1A1E24`):** Deep carbon typography on pristine neutral surfaces.

### Surface Tiers
- **Surface Canvas (`#FFFFFF`):** Gallery-grade crisp white for primary editorial body sections.
- **Surface Muted (`#F6F8FA`):** Soft architectural limestone tint for off-canvas cards, subtle pill backdrops, and secondary section bands.
- **Surface Translucent (`rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.72)`): Frosted glass for floating navigation bars, stat pills, and hero badges with backdrop blur.
- **Surface Dark Overlay (`rgba(13, 17, 23, 0.85)`): Deep scrims on full-bleed imagery providing contrast for white typography and pill controls.

## Typography

The typography strategy leverages deliberate editorial contrast:

- **The Editorial Hook (`Playfair Display`):** Employed for evocative lead phrasing, poetic prefixes (e.g., italic *Where*, *vision of*, *Purpose*), giving headlines a magazine cover aesthetic.
- **The Structural Backbone (`Plus Jakarta Sans`):** Clean, crisp geometric sans rendered in uppercase or high weights (`LIFE BELONGS`) to juxtapose the romantic serif with architectural precision.
- **Stat & Meta Tier:** Numeric counters feature tight negative tracking with paired condensed uppercase sub-labels (`PROPERTIES`, `YEARS EXPERIENCE`).

## Layout & Spacing

The layout model is built on a 12-column fluid grid system paired with centered max-width content containers (`1280px`).

### Spatial Principles
- **Hero Canopy:** Top sections employ a sweeping architectural viewport with an organic curved bottom shelf or inverted tab cutout.
- **Asymmetric Masonry:** Content blocks alternate between expansive full-span cards and clustered 7:5 / 8:4 column arrangements for property showcases.
- **Rhythm & Bleed:** Margins on desktop are generous (`4.5rem` to `6rem` vertical padding between editorial acts) to let architectural forms breathe against pure white backdrops.
- **Mobile Reflow:** 12-column layouts compress into single-column vertical stacks. Horizontal multi-card clusters collapse into edge-to-edge scroll viewports or compact stacked rounded blocks with preserved corner radii (`1.5rem`).

## Elevation & Depth

Visual hierarchy uses architectural layering, glassmorphic floatation, and atmospheric backdrops:

### Layering Levels
- **Tier 0 (Environmental Backdrop):** Organic gradient skies and dimensional landscape mesh acting as the primary backdrop horizon.
- **Tier 1 (Base Shell):** High-radius white and nocturne architectural enclosures (`rounded-[2.5rem]`) floating with soft boundary shadows (`0 20px 50px rgba(0, 0, 0, 0.08)`).
- **Tier 2 (Glassmorphism & Badges):** Translucent pills and counter pods sitting directly on media layers. Styled with `rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.65)` backdrops, 16px blur, hairline inset light rings (`inset 0 1px 0 rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.4)`), and faint drop shadows (`0 8px 24px rgba(0, 0, 0, 0.06)`).
- **Tier 3 (Floating Controls):** Nocturne navigation capsules and primary CTA buttons utilizing deep tonal isolation (`#0D1117`) to create foreground contrast.

## Shapes

The interface embraces organic curvature alongside geometric precision:

- **Pill Geometry (Level 3):** Buttons, navigation bars, stat modules, and category tags utilize full capsule pill shapes (`rounded-full` / `9999px`).
- **Architectural Curvature:** Large display cards, image viewports, and modal sheets feature generous radii (`2rem` to `2.5rem`), referencing modern organic architecture.
- **Notched Cutouts:** Select primary cards feature distinctive concave bottom notches or tab transitions where action pills dock seamlessly into the frame silhouette.

## Components

### Buttons
- **Primary Pill:** Background `#0D1117` or `#EE4D2D`, text `#FFFFFF`, rounded `9999px`, height `48px`, horizontal padding `24px`. Often accompanied by a trailing circular micro-icon container.
- **Secondary Frosted Pill:** Translucent background (`rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.85)`), text `#0D1117`, hairline border `rgba(0, 0, 0, 0.06)`, backdrop blur `12px`.
- **Outline Minimal:** Transparent background, `1.5px` border in `#0D1117` or `#EE4D2D`, uppercase label font.

### Navigation Bar
- **Floating Island:** Detached capsule centered at the top viewport. Translucent frosted body, inner pill links with subtle hover fills, and an embedded right-aligned micro-action button.

### Cards & Modules
- **Architectural Hero Card:** Full-bleed photograph, top rounded corners `2.5rem`, bottom notched cutout framing a docked CTA capsule.
- **Stat Counter Pills:** Stacked vertical floating cards over imagery featuring bold count values (`250+`, `15+`) atop muted uppercase subtext (`Properties`, `Years Experience`).
- **Dark Architectural Grid Cards:** Dark `#0D1117` containers housing secondary photography, hairline borders, and subtle bottom label rows detailing property name and geographical coordinates.

### Inputs & Interactive Elements
- **Search & Filter Capsules:** Minimal pill-shaped inputs with left-aligned monoline icons and inline pill submission triggers.
- **Checkboxes & Radios:** Circular indicators; selected state filled with primary `#EE4D2D` containing a crisp white center pip or checkmark.
